Évora 2027: what was promised, what the panel wrote, and the money line by line

The limits of this document

This document puts three things side by side: what Évora's bid for European Capital of Culture said it would do, what the European Commission's expert panel wrote about it and on what date, and what money is written into public acts, by whom and for what.

The panel judges, this project does not. The expert panel has a published mandate and writes dated reports; O Estado do País quotes them. There is no delivery status here, no colours, no tally of promises kept against promises broken. Every cell names the document, the page and the date.

Only a commitment carrying a figure or a deadline enters as a promise. A commitment with no value and no date cannot be checked, and this document says so rather than turning it into a judgement.

The three cost aggregates do not agree, and no document bridges them. The panel writes one overall budget; the bid book writes another; the cooperation protocol of June 2023, as the association's activity plan splits it, writes a third. Each enters this document with the construction its own source declares, and never added together. Adding items from different documents would manufacture a figure: the sources overlap, they use different reference years, and they mix what is planned with what is paid.

Every figure is written the way its own document writes it. That is why two thousands separators and two spellings of the same value appear: the bid book writes a space, the association's activity plan writes a dot, and this document standardises neither. Where an official document contradicts itself, both readings stay, side by side, with no choice made between them.

Co-financing rates do not enter. The published sheet for one of the European operations writes one percentage and the managing authority's list writes another for the same operation, and neither document says on what base it is computed. What enters is the amount in euros.

The sources are closed. Everything here comes from the 53 reads registered on 2026-09-15 and listed one by one at the end; 43 answered and 10 did not. Nothing else was read and nothing else is quoted: no press, no encyclopaedias, no site outside that list. What is missing is stated in the section on what was not found. Quotations stay in the language the source wrote them in, in both editions of this document: the European panel writes in English and the acts of the Portuguese State write in Portuguese, and translating either would be rewriting it.

What this document does not answer. What Évora 2027 has cost in total. What contracts the association signed and with whom. How much the municipality actually transferred. What the 2027 programme is. Whether the Melina Mercouri Prize will be paid. None of these has an answer in the documents read, and each one carries its reason further down.

What the bid promised, and what the panel wrote

A promise is matched to a judgement here only by the object both documents name: a venue, a budget line, the team, contracting, the overall budget, the prize. Nothing is inferred. Where the panel wrote nothing about an object, the cell says so; where the panel's judgement has no matching promise, the bid cell says which pages were searched.

The same decree-law obliges the association to publish «A identidade e os elementos curriculares de todos os membros dos seus órgãos sociais, designadamente do órgão de administração, bem como as respetivas remunerações e outros benefícios.». No document read carries them.

Decree-law 47/2025 added public-interest status to it, with an end date: «é atribuído o estatuto de utilidade pública à Associação até dia 31 de dezembro de 2028.»
