Estudo de identidade · v2 Observatório · 12.08.2026

One system, built to your rule.

v1 set four candidates side by side. This is one system. It keeps the parts you responded to — the metrological voice of board A, the 308-municipality constellation from Observatório 308 — and rebuilds them under the rule you set: instruments, not illustrations.

Nothing here is drawn for effect. Every graphic measures something, and every graphic carries its own depth in place: the number at a glance, one sentence of meaning, then method, caveats and provenance — unfolding inside the same graphic, never behind another page. Motion appears only where it encodes a change of state.

The name is the one decision still open, so both are set here in the same system, at the same sizes, with nothing else to separate them.

  • Legibility Didot is out. Both wordmarks are set in a high-legibility old-style serif, and each board reports the face and size it actually rendered at, alongside fixed 17 px and 11 px proofs — the sizes a masthead has to survive in nav and in a footer.
  • Ornament v1's tick-strip is removed. Ticks now appear only inside instruments, on axes that carry values.
  • The 308 The dots are real CAOP 2025 centroids, one per municipality. The silhouette is not drawn — it falls out of the coordinates. Nothing is placed by eye.
A decisão em aberto

Two names, one treatment

Same face, same weight, same furniture, same proof sizes. The only variable is the name.

Signals a standing report on the condition of the country — civic, editorial, durable enough to head a yearly edition. It reads as a publication rather than a database, which suits a title that has to survive being cited.

Risk: estado carries the State as well as the state of things, so the title can be read as government output — a real hazard for an observatory whose independence is the product. It is also a common enough phrase that owning it, in search and in trademark, will be work.

17 pxPortugal, Medidonavegação
11 pxPortugal, Medidorodapé

Signals method before opinion. The comma does the work: it makes the title a claim about how the country is treated, not a claim about the country. It also states the promise the rest of the system has to keep.

Risk: it promises measurement on every page, so any qualitative, narrative or interview piece reads as off-brand. Medido also means restrained, which can land as tentative rather than rigorous.

Three faces, three jobs, no overlap: the serif appears only in wordmarks, the mono carries every number and label, the sans carries prose only. That separation is the identity — not an ornament laid on top of it.

O que o número quer dizer

O índice compara o PIB per capita de cada território, medido em paridades de poder de compra, com a média da UE-27 fixada em 100. Um valor de 82 significa 18% abaixo dessa média; 129 significa 29% acima.

Distâncias

As diferenças em pontos que a régua desenha (por exemplo −18 ou +29) são calculadas a partir dos valores publicados. São aritmética sobre esses valores, não valores publicados em si.
