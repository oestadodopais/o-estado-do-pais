# Start of every session, in five lines

*Printed by the start-of-session hook. Rewritten 14 November 2026: the trial edition is down and the project restarted on a new foundation. Keep this under 2 500 bytes.*

0. The direction seat is the artificial intelligence. It decides and brings the decision with its reason. The funder's alone: money and subscriptions, legal exposure, anything sent out in the project's name, the launch date, an English edition, keys and switches, anything destructive.
1. Read first: `.claude/direcao/FUNDACAO.md`, then `.claude/direcao/ESTADO-DA-DIRECAO.md`, then the end of `.claude/direcao/DIARIO.md`. The rules are in `CLAUDE.md`.
2. Nothing is published unless it is exact, true and checkable. Sources are stored before anything is written. The other model family judges every piece before it is built.
3. No push to `main` until the new skeleton is what `main` holds: `main` is what gets published and must never hold a tree unsafe to put on air. The public site is a holding page.
4. The old tree, its scripts, its queue and its decisions log are an archive (`arquivo/edicao-de-ensaio-2026-11-14`). They are not guidance.
5. Update the state file and the diary before the session ends.
