# Start of every session, in five lines

*Printed by the start-of-session hook. Rewritten 17 November 2026, when the project stopped building to choose its concept. Keep this under 2 500 bytes.*

0. The project is choosing its concept. Nothing is being built. Do not resume any earlier order of work.
1. Read first, each whole: `.claude/direcao/IDEIA.md` (what the project is for), `PENSAMENTO.md` (the concept, with reasons), `ESTADO-DA-DIRECAO.md` (the next step), then the last five entries of `DIARIO.md`. The rules are in `CLAUDE.md`.
2. The direction seat is the artificial intelligence. It brings propositions of its own and agrees the concept with the funder. The funder's alone: money and subscriptions, legal exposure, anything sent out in the project's name, an English edition, keys and switches, anything destructive.
3. Nothing is published unless it is exact, true and checkable. `main` moves only by fast-forward to a head with `portao` green. A push publishes nothing. The public site is a holding page.
4. `FUNDACAO.md`, the diary and everything archived are records of earlier editions. They are not guidance.
5. Before the session ends: update the state page, add one short diary entry, and run the cold-start test the state page describes.
