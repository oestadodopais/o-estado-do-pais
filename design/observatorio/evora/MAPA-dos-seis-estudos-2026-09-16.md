## 3. What a reader hits

**Questions spread across studies** `[F]`:

- *How much PRR money does Évora have, and how much arrived?* Three studies, three snapshot dates, three figure sets (rows 1 and 2). No study says which snapshot is current.
- *What does Évora 2027 cost and who pays?* Five studies touch it: `evora-2027` (the promise and the acts), `orcamentado` (the budget lines and the 2025 zero), `quinze-anos` (the 2026 plan), `prometido` (PRR component C04 and the association's contracts), `economia` (the seed, the aviso, the 25 million, the tourism stress test). Four numbers come out of one GOP 2026 (row 8).
- *What does Évora spend on culture?* Three definitions across two studies (row 14), plus association spending in `evora-2027`.
- *Who is accountable?* `pelouros` says the pelouro is political and the accounts refuse it; `prometido` says "O endereço da responsabilização, na maior parte dos casos, não são os paços do concelho"; `quinze-anos` says a party owns decisions, not curves. Three answers, each correct in its own frame `[J]`.
- *The hospital.* Four studies, four money objects, no join (row 10).

**Where a figure appears twice with different values or dates** `[F]`: rows 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 13, 14, 15, 16, 19, 20. The sharpest are the PRR triple (row 1), the São Bento de Cástris triple (row 4) and the earnings series end year (row 15).

**What is missing although the pieces exist** `[J]`:

1. **The câmara's own PRR line against its own accounts.** `prometido` has `€12 069 013` contracted and `€2 047 336` paid to the Município; `orcamentado` has `€1 087 287` of co-financed capital in 2025. Nobody puts the two side by side, which is the only honest version of "what the câmara did with the recovery money".
2. **A crosswalk for the 2027 works.** One building appears in the bid book, the PRR register, the association's plan and the municipal annex, under four names, promoters and values. Every piece exists; the join does not.
3. **The association's contracts.** `prometido` lists 39 contracts worth `€1 997 619`; `evora-2027`, written later, declares the question unanswerable.
4. **Who holds the CEC politically.** `pelouros` shows "Comércio e Turismo" unattributed in both the mandate that won the title and the one that will run the year, and culture split between two holders in every mandate. No study asks who answers for the 2027 programme inside the câmara.
5. **Capacity against the 2027 stress test.** `economia` names the short stay (1,7 nights) and the coming demand; no study measures beds, rooms or the works that would absorb it.

**One structural judgment** `[J]`: the six studies are not fragments of one study. They are six correct answers to six different questions that happen to share a place, and the fracture the director feels is not in the prose but in the keys. Nothing joins them because nothing in them is keyed: no shared snapshot date, no shared entity identifier, no shared building or project identifier, no shared definition of culture, investment or debt. The grouping above is worth doing only if those keys are built first; regrouping the prose without them would move the seams, not close them.
