In the speech published by the PCP, Paulo Raimundo states that the budget allocates 1 700 million euros to road public-private partnerships, in these words: «Vem com um orçamento que entrega 1 700 milhões às parcerias público-privadas rodoviárias». In the Budget report for 2027 (PDF p. 123), Table 4.25 forecasts gross road charges of 1 152 million euros for 2027: what the public partner (the State) pays to road partnerships that year, with availability and service payments, the costs of toll collection, major repairs and compensation to private partners. Net charges are 889 million: what it pays after deducting revenue, which is the tolls collected on tolled roads (except where they belong to the private partner), the benefit or revenue sharing provided for in the contracts and the fixed payments by subconcessionaires (the private companies that operate the roads), among the main components the report lists on PDF p. 124. The stated amount is higher than both the gross and net charges printed in the table for 2027.
