## afirmacao-pcp-ppp-rodoviarias-oe2027-2026-10-09 · EN

In the speech published by the PCP, Paulo Raimundo states that the budget allocates 1 700 million euros to road public-private partnerships, in these words: «Vem com um orçamento que entrega 1 700 milhões às parcerias público-privadas rodoviárias». In the Budget report for 2027 (PDF p. 123), Table 4.25 forecasts gross road charges of 1 152 million euros for 2027: what the public partner (the State) pays to road partnerships that year, with availability and service payments, the costs of toll collection, major repairs and compensation to private partners. Net charges are 889 million: what it pays after deducting revenue, which is the tolls collected on tolled roads (except where they belong to the private partner), the benefit or revenue sharing provided for in the contracts and the fixed payments by subconcessionaires (the private companies that operate the roads), among the main components the report lists on PDF p. 124. The stated amount is higher than both the gross and net charges printed in the table for 2027.

## oe-2027-ppp-rodoviario-encargos-liquidos-2027 · PT

Os encargos brutos são o que o parceiro público (o Estado) paga às parcerias rodoviárias nesse ano: os pagamentos pela disponibilidade das vias, os pagamentos por serviço indexados ao tráfego, os gastos com a cobrança das portagens, as grandes reparações de pavimentos a cargo do parceiro público e as compensações a pagar aos parceiros privados, entre as principais componentes que o relatório lista na p. 124 do PDF. As receitas são o que o parceiro público recebe nessas parcerias: as portagens cobradas nas vias portajadas (exceto nas parcerias em que as portagens pertencem ao parceiro privado), a partilha de benefícios ou de receitas prevista nos contratos, e os pagamentos fixos das subconcessionárias (as empresas privadas que exploram as vias) ao parceiro público. Os encargos líquidos são os encargos brutos menos as receitas: 1 152 menos 263 dá 889, em milhões de euros. São valores de caixa, o dinheiro que sai e que entra no ano, em euros de 2027.

## oe-2027-ppp-rodoviario-encargos-liquidos-2027 · EN

Gross charges are what the public partner (the State) pays to the road partnerships in that year: availability payments for the roads, service payments indexed to traffic, the costs of toll collection, major pavement repairs borne by the public partner and compensation payable to private partners, among the main components the report lists on PDF p. 124. Revenue is what the public partner receives in those partnerships: the tolls collected on tolled roads (except in partnerships where the tolls belong to the private partner), the benefit or revenue sharing provided for in the contracts, and the fixed payments made by subconcessionaires (the private companies that operate the roads) to the public partner. Net charges are gross charges minus revenue: 1 152 minus 263 gives 889, in millions of euros. These are cash values, the money paid and received in the year, in 2027 euros.
