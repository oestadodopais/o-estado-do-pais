The conclusion

The three claims correspond to different lines of the table and none presents all three measures together. In 2027, Health is budgeted to receive 267,5 million eurossource · calculated less than the expenditure estimate for 2026 because the RRP ends, and expenditure outside the RRP rises 511,8source · calculated against the 771,1source · calculated the RRP leaves. Personnel expenditure rises, while the purchase of goods and services and investment fall. Since 2020, no initial Health budget in the years with completed execution has reached the end of the year without being exceeded, and the 2027 one is below what is estimated to be spent in 2026. For those who use the National Health Service, the budget for 2027 is more money for the people who work in it and less for what is bought and built, at a time when expenditure on medicines is higher than a year ago, the surgery waiting list has grown and around half of the contracted RRP health amount is still unpaid.

The recommendation

The useful question is not whether the Health budget rises or falls, but whether it is enough: the answer of the last six years is that it never was, and the execution to August 2026, 19,9 %source · Entidade Orçamental above 2025, reinforces the risk of another shortfall. Whoever follows Health in 2027 watches the Entidade Orçamental's monthly budget execution summary and the possible need for reinforcement, not just the approval. They also follow the National Health Service's monthly data: users without a family doctor, the surgery waiting list and the debt to suppliers say more about 2027 than the vote.
