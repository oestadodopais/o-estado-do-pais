
## What this block is

The second piece of block B1 of a surface rebuild of a live Astro static site ("O Estado do País", a Portuguese observatory of official statistics; Portuguese edition at `/`, English at `/en/`). This piece is "the places". It must deliver: (1) two declared tables, each with a gate and a planted fault: the theme of every municipal measure, and the region and district of every municipality READ FROM the three official CAOP 2025 extracts the project hosts (`public/dados/caop-2025-municipios-*.csv`, columns `dtmn`, `distrito_ilha`, `nuts2`), never typed by hand (308 municipalities, 29 districts and islands, 9 regions; six districts span two regions, so a municipality's line states its own region and its own district); (2) the place page for all 308 municipalities (`src/views/MunicipioView.astro`) by the Évora mock-up: the line "Portugal › region › district › municipality" with each part linked, the name, a short reading, the numbers grouped by theme with the existing card anatomy, the studies about the place composed from the studies registry (no hand-written list), "O que mudou" with dated lines, and nothing else; (3) a new places page (`/lugares/`, `/en/places/`) with the map, the municipality search, and two plain lists (the nine regions; the 29 districts and islands), the three old index routes redirected with 301 in `vercel.json`, and the menu's three place entries reduced to one; (4) two explanatory sentences cut from the region and district pages; (5) the phrase inventory for these page families reduced to a closed list of labels plus data strings, with every removed string recorded with its reason.

The reading of a place is the editorially sensitive part. For Évora it is a sentence written by the editorial seat: «A câmara de Évora deve menos do que a lei lhe permite, e o índice de dívida caiu para menos de metade em dez anos: de 242,6 % em 2014 para 105,5 % em 2024.» (English: «Évora's council owes less than the law allows, and its debt index has fallen to less than half in ten years: from 242.6% in 2014 to 105.5% in 2024.»), with both values sealed from ledger rows. For the other 307 it is composed from two comparisons the ledger allows for every municipality, using words the project already uses: the council's debt index against the legal limit ("dentro do limite legal" / "fora do limite legal"; the limit is the row `indice-de-divida-limite-legal`, 150) and purchasing power per person against the national average (above or below the base of the index). A comparison whose value is not published drops out of the sentence (Penedono's debt index is "N.d."); if both drop, the place has no reading. Population stays on its card, not in the reading. No computed differences that have no ledger row. A number that is in the reading must not open the first row of its theme. Ten councils are outside the legal limit (Vila Real de Santo António is the highest, 419,5).

House rules that bind the builder: no number typed by hand, every figure on a page resolves to a ledger row and carries its seal with the unit inside the seal; no sentence that explains the page, the project, the method or the source; one door per thing; the site's vocabulary (the project is never «a casa» nor «o sítio»; a subject is «tema»; a place is «lugar», «concelho», «distrito», «ilha», «região»); new prose in European Portuguese without dashes; a gate is never weakened in what it protects: a cell that encoded the old page's furniture may change shape keeping what it protects, with a planted fault proving it still bites; the three full gates run once, on the final head, each in its own command with its exit code written to a file.

## What the package contains

`brief.md` (the block brief with its 21.09 amendment, section 6, which prevails), `relatorio-construtor.md` (the builder's report), `diff.patch` (base `0cfd9832` to the builder's head), the changed files at their paths, `built/` with built pages from the builder's head (the place pages of Évora, Vila Real de Santo António, Penedono, Espinho and Corvo in Portuguese and Évora and Espinho in English; `/lugares/` in both editions; one region page and one district page), `antes/` with the same Évora page and the old municipalities index from the base, the three CAOP extracts (`public/dados/`), the ledger rows those five municipalities use (`ledger/claims/`), and the gate exit-code files and logs the report cites.

## Pay particular attention to

1. **Every number on the five built place pages against the ledger rows in the package**: value, unit, period, and that the seal opens the right row; the two comparisons of each reading against the rows (is "dentro"/"fora do limite legal" right for that municipality's index against 150? is "acima"/"abaixo da média do país" right for its purchasing-power index against the base?); Penedono must have no debt clause; nothing computed that has no row.
2. **The region and district of each of the five municipalities against the CAOP extracts**, and whether the table really is read from the extracts at build time (not a typed copy); whether the gate would fail for a municipality without a region, a region of the map without an entry, or counts other than 308, 29 and 9; whether its planted faults prove that.
3. **The line of Espinho** (district Aveiro, region Norte): each part must be true for Espinho and linked to an existing page; nothing on any page may state or imply that a district belongs to a single region.
4. **Every name, label or definition from outside the project** (item 6 of the common instructions): the card names and the one-sentence definitions on the place pages against the rows they are attached to.
5. **Sentences that explain**, second doors to the same thing, empty cells, a measure shown twice, words outside the site's vocabulary, English calques in the Portuguese, Portuguese left in the English edition.
6. **The gate cells the builder reshaped or retired**: for each, what it protected before, what it protects now, whether the planted fault proves it still bites, and whether anything that protected a number, a source or a person (including the AI disclosure, which is a legal protection) was weakened.
7. **The redirects**: the six old index routes, their exact targets, 301, no leftover files for the old routes in the build, and links across the site that still point to the old routes.
8. **An editor's question, asked of each built page in Portuguese**: would the editor of a Portuguese daily print this page as it is? Say where not, and why, in one line per page.
