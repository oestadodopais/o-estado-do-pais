# [C001] Évora 2027: the June 2025 report to the panel put transfers at 34 million; the year-end accounts report 9,7 million received under the protocol and list the municipality's share as «Em Negociação» (under negotiation)

[C002] *Évora 2027, European Capital of Culture: what the bid promised, what the European panel wrote and the money in the official acts*

## [C003] Does Évora 2027 have the money, the works and the programme it promised?

[C004] On the money, the accounts of the Associação Évora 2027 declare 9,7 million received from the 2023 protocol up to the end of 2025. In June 2025, the summary that the panel of European experts makes of the progress report gave 34 million as already transferred. The municipality's share, of 10,5 million, was «Em Negociação» in the accounts approved in April 2026. On the works, the Câmara wrote on 23 October 2026 that the second Rossio de São Brás works will not be finished that year. The opening is set there, on 6 February 2027. On the programme, the association's news page did not announce the 2027 one on 3 November 2026.

[C005] The association writes that in 2025 it reopened dialogue with the Câmara to obtain the money it needs to honour its commitments to artistic, cultural, educational and social bodies.

## [C006] What we conclude

[C007] 1. **Under the protocol, the accounts report 9,7 million received by the end of 2025; the report to the panel put the figure at 34.** The accounts report 2 million received in 2024 and, paid in 2025, 3,75 million from culture and 3,92 million from tourism; the association's plan dates the tourism payment to the end of the year. They also report a further 1,5 million paid in 2025 for the Linha do Vagar, outside the 34 million, an addition the bid did not envisage. The difference between the 34 and the 9,7 million is not all money in arrears. The resolution sets base ceilings for culture of 3,75 million in 2026 and 5,5 million in 2027, which add up to 9,25 million and can grow with the previous year's balance. The association's plan forecasts receipt in 2026 of the 80 000 euros still outstanding from tourism. The 15 million from the Ministério da Coesão Territorial consists of European funding that must be applied for: in the June 2026 list of the Alentejo 2030 regional programme, the funding approved for the association adds up to 4,25 million.

[C008] 2. **The municipality's 10,5 million is listed as a commitment «Em Negociação» in the association's 2025 accounts.** Those accounts recognise no revenue from the municipality in 2025, and the 2026 plan states «Sem valores confirmados» (no confirmed amounts) in its forecast for that year. The bid envisaged 2 million in 2025 and 2 002 750 euros in 2026. The municipality's 2024 multi-year planning document forecast 2,5 million for the association in 2025. The 2026 document lists 1 000† euros for 2026. These are forecasts made in different years, not payments. The panel's summary records that municipal funds covered the pre-operational phase. At the selection stage, the panel praised the municipality's decision to double its contribution.

[C009] 3. **The contract for the second construction project at the Rossio, where the opening is scheduled to take place, was awarded a hundred days beforehand.** There are two construction projects at the Rossio de São Brás. The first is subject to the Plano de Recuperação e Resiliência deadlines; the second, the Câmara says, is funded entirely by the Fundo Ambiental. On 14 May 2026, the mayor said at a meeting that the second project, if the funding application were approved, would be completed before the opening. On 23 October, the Câmara wrote that it would not be completed in 2026; the notice gives no new date. According to the panel's account, the team said in June 2025 that seven venues had recovery plan funding and would be completed by August 2026. It also said temporary venues were available if needed. At the end of 2025, the association's accounts listed two of the seven as «Sem desenvolvimento» (no progress). The association is not responsible for carrying out the works: it writes that it has neither the legal powers nor budgeted funds to do so. Of the seven recovery plan projects, the accounts assign five to the municipality, one to Património Cultural and one to the regional tourism body. The Centro Cultural and the dance centre, with costs estimated in the bid at 12 and 3 million, were unfunded or delayed in June 2025, according to the panel's summary of the progress report. The same summary says the team identified alternatives, such as repurposing existing spaces and using temporary venues.

[C010] 4. **According to the panel's account, the team expected all contracts to be signed by September 2025; the accounts record 13 that year.** These were 13 projects listed in the bid book and contracted during 2025; the accounts record a further 15 for which preparations for contracting were complete. In June 2025, the panel's summary referred to a portfolio of 171 projects. The two counts relate to different dates and measure different things: one counts projects in the portfolio, the other projects contracted during a year. The association recorded costs of 1,8 million in 2025 and forecasts expenditure of 19,7 million for 2026, about eleven times that amount. According to the panel's account, the team said the full programme would be announced in September 2026. The association's plan schedules the programme presentation for November in the text and October in the heading of the same item. As of 3 November, the association's news page had not announced it; the latest news item was dated 11 August.

[C011] 5. **The protocol allocating the funding was not found on the official pages reviewed, and the totals do not match.** The bid states revenue of 45,8 million; the report to the panel gives an overall budget of 49,5 million; and the commitments table in the 2025 accounts gives 46,3 million. The three figures differ in date, scope and composition, and no document reviewed reconciles them. The association attributes an increase of about 5 million over the bid figures to inflation. The commitments table prints a total of 46 259 953 euros; its rows add up to 46 459 953. In that table, the association lists 30,9 million as contracted or approved. This includes 10 million for the VAGAR operation, although the detailed accounts identify only 1,75 million of that amount as approved. The decree-law requires disclosure of the remuneration of members of the governing bodies, and no document reviewed provides it.

## [C012] What is right, what is wrong

[C013] **What is right.**

[C014] - The accounts declare culture payments equal to the resolution's ceilings for 2024 and 2025: 2 million and 3,75 million.
[C015] - Tourism paid 3,92 of its 4 million, and the Government added 1,5 million for a national call for projects and artistic commissions.
[C016] - The association published its 2025 accounts, with the sole auditor's opinion, and that year opened three of the bid's calls for projects.
[C017] - The panel's summary of the progress report gives the Salão Central Eborense as finished. The panel writes, in the June 2025 report, that Évora 2027 is «on the right track».
[C018] - There are contingency plans. The panel's summary refers to temporary venues, repurposing existing spaces and adaptable projects; the accounts refer to unconventional spaces, outdoor settings and additional sessions.

[C019] **What is wrong.**

[C020] - By the panel's summary of the progress report, the association only became fully operational in October 2024, almost two years after the selection. The accounts date the artistic director's start to 22 April 2025.
[C021] - The recovery plan allocation for the works fell from 26 million to 16,3 million over 2025, by the association's accounts.
[C022] - In the accounts table, the regional programme's culture funding line, for the VAGAR operation, lists 10 million as approved. The detailed figures in the same accounts and the programme's June 2026 list record approved funding of 1,75 million; the accounts describe the remainder as «programado» (programmed).
[C023] - The association writes that the territory does not have enough infrastructure to present the whole programme.
[C024] - The panel recommended finalising all funding commitments by the end of 2025; in the accounts approved on 14 April 2026, the municipality's share was listed as «Em Negociação».

## [C025] Who it matters to

[C026] **For those with a project in the bid.** The association writes that the municipality's share serves to honour its commitments to artistic, cultural, educational and social bodies. The accounts record 13 bid projects contracted during 2025.

[C027] **For artists and cultural associations in the Alentejo.** The bid gave 2 million to the «A Nossa Vez» call, for about 30 projects. The call opened on 10 November 2025 with 1,3 million. The accounts speak, in general terms, of new editions of the calls, without saying which or with what budget.

[C028] **For schools.** «A Nossa Voz» opened on 15 September 2025 for the 18 school clusters in Alentejo Central and two private schools, with 400 000 euros. The bid allocated 500 000 to it.

[C029] **For those who live in Évora or plan to go there in 2027.** The association's plan expects the programme to run from 6 February to 7 December 2027. The full 2027 programme was not found on the association's pages read on 15 September and 3 November 2026, nor was an estimate of visitors found in the documents read. The May 2026 minutes record only the mayor's reference to studies that point to a possible doubling.

## [C038] Our reading

[C039] The culture share is paid up to the 2024 and 2025 ceilings; the municipality's is still to be confirmed, and part of the European money depends on applications.

[C040] Our reading is that the report made to the European panel in June 2025 counted as money transferred what was money promised. The 34 million is the State's share, national and European, that the Câmara announced for the protocol in June 2023, to which the municipality's contribution is added.

[C041] What is right is that the culture payments declared for 2024 and 2025 follow those years' ceilings, and that the association published accounts with the sole auditor's opinion. What is wrong is that the contribution the panel praised at the selection, the municipality's, is the one the association's accounts give as «Em Negociação».

[C042] For those with a project in the bid, uncertainty over the municipality's share puts commitments in the programme at risk. For those who live in Évora, the 1,5 million European prize is conditional on the city honouring the promises made in its bid; the panel makes its recommendation at the end of 2026.

## [C043] The recommendations

[C044] 1. **To the Câmara and the Assembleia Municipal de Évora:** publish the protocol of 21 June 2023 and specify in the 2027 budget how much will be transferred to the association and when, before the opening on 6 February 2027. At the end of 2026, the panel will recommend whether the prize should be paid; one condition is that the bid's promises are honoured. Readers can find the forecast in the 2027 budget and multi-year plan. Payments can be checked against the municipality's budget execution records and the association's accounts for the years in which they occur.

[C045] 2. **To the Associação Évora 2027:** publish with the programme, by the end of 2026, a table of the money by source and by year, with what was promised, contracted and received, and the pay of the governing bodies. The panel recommended «maintain financial transparency»; the table would say how the three totals, which have different dates, scopes and compositions, are reconciled. The reader checks it on the association's «Quem Somos» (About us) page.

[C046] 3. **To the Câmara Municipal de Évora:** say by the end of 2026 when the second Rossio de São Brás works will be finished and where the opening ceremony will be held if the square is not ready. The 23 October notice says the works will not be finished in 2026 and gives no new date. The reader follows the decisions in the notices of the council meetings; the date of the works and the place of the opening require an announcement by the Câmara.
