Executed expenditure on general public services (central administration, consolidated effective expenditure)

The name at the source «Execução Orçamental de julho 2026 - despesa por classificação funcional»

What this number is

It is what central administration spent on this function from January to July, under the classification of expenditure by function, in consolidated effective expenditure: spending without transactions in financial assets and liabilities or transfers between its own services. [a verificar] · a field not confirmed against the source, not a doubt about what is published

20 258,7 million euros, cumulative January to July

The source file does not print the unit; the scale in million euros was checked against revenue, expenditure and the balance in the July summary, with six exact matches. Consolidated effective expenditure excludes financial assets and liabilities and internal transfers between the services covered. Adding the functions requires the consolidation difference and may differ from the total because of rounding in the source. The budget maps and budget execution use public budget accounting and the scope stated in the name. Eurostat uses national accounts and also includes regional and local government. These measures are not directly comparable.
