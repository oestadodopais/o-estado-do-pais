The name at the source Harmonised index of consumer prices (HICP) - ECOICOP ver.2 - indices and rates of change, monthly data

What this series is

It is how much, in Portugal, rent prices changed compared with the same month of the previous year, in the harmonised measure used to compare the countries of the European Union (harmonised index of consumer prices).

Periods January 1997 to August 2026 · Frequency monthly · %

identifier serie-ihpc-rendas-variacao-homologa

Published by Eurostat, in «Harmonised index of consumer prices (HICP) - ECOICOP ver.2 - indices and rates of change, monthly data» (prc_hicp_minr, freq=M, unit=RCH_A, coicop18=CP041, geo=PT).

In August 2026, the value was 5,2 %: more than in the same month a year earlier, August 2025, when it was 5,1 %.
