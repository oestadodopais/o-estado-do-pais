Findings that shaped the direction: colour-as-state has Portuguese precedent (DN 1995, Público 2000, Polígrafo) but every hue has a party echo, so colour enters by norm (ISO 3864) and material; «value against a fixed reference» has a full grammar (Plimsoll, control charts, bullet graph, ONS 2px reference rule, MIP thresholds never composed into an index, Marégrafo de Cascais as the national zero); the Portuguese form of the fixed-pattern instrument is the type specimen (Imprensa Nacional catalogues since 1838) and the boletim de voto; Portuguese foundries with licences (Feliciano Standard, perpetual, print+web, Small ≤100k visitors/month, €50 a style); the AI-generic tics named with sources. Gaps recorded honestly: no foundry confirms tabular figures; no Portuguese screen palette; no Portuguese statistical-office design norm found.
- **Direction «Espécime» decided** (canvas https://claude.ai/code/artifact/3ea5c63c-635b-4135-9086-3f8767389585, page «Direção»; file `oedp-especime/direcao.md`): a fixed sheet in ink on cool lime-white paper where only the measured thing changes; the reference always engraved; colour only as state. Grammar: the régua-espécime, ink instruments, the receipt as boletim, the seal at line height, the time signal as the only masthead colour. Contrasts measured; state never by colour alone.
