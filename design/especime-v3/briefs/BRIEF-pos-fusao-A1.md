The seat pushes the bundle with DesignSync afterwards; you do not.

**Discipline, unchanged from v2 (read the file's header comment and keep every rule of it):** the cards are portraits of what exists, extracted from `dist/` and the stylesheets; nothing is designed here; no colour literal enters the generator (every swatch is painted with the token and the value printed is read from `tokens.css`); numbers of layouts are read from the stylesheets and the run dies if the form changed; no `<script>`, no data islands; no request out; the bundle folder is generated and gitignored; the run checks every card and exits 1 if one fails. What is copy in a card (the sentences explaining a rule) quotes `IDENTIDADE.md` or `direcao.md` word for word or says where it comes from; a sentence you cannot trace gets `[verify]` and goes to the report.
