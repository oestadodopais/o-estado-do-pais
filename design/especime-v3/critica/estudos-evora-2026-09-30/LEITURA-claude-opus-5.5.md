## Diagnosis

**The six**
- **Orçamentado, Pago, Devido 2025**: what did the câmara budget, collect, pay and owe in 2025? It collected 61.44 % of its budget (96 % in 2021). The squeeze is on suppliers (137 days to pay, €4,976,172 overdue), not on legal debt. The 2024 accounts were never certified. Period: 2025.
- **Quinze Anos, Cinco Mandatos**: what did each executive inherit, decide and leave? A rescue-era debt that took years to measure (€82.9M, then €95.1M), paid down for a decade, and stalled in the last mandate. Period: 2010–2025.
- **Os Pelouros**: who held which portfolio, and what did each area spend? The president's list holds every portfolio, and only two people held them in 2021–2025. No source publishes money per portfolio. Period: 2009–2025.
- **Prometido, Pago, Auditado 2026**: who holds the PRR money attributed to Évora, and what is overdue, contracted and audited? 61.32 % of approved money sits in overdue projects; 51.95 % is paid. The university holds more than the câmara. Period: August 2026.
- **Economia, Investidores, Portas Abertas 2026**: what does the concelho produce, who invested, and which funding doors were open? A richer city (111.47) in a poorer region (93.86). Four firms hold 21.5 % of business value (2.56 % nationally). Period: 2022–2025; doors as of 2026-08-10.
- **Évora 2027**: what did the Capital of Culture bid promise, what did the EU panel write, and what money do public acts show? State money arrived; the municipality's €10.5M has not. The big venues are unfunded or delayed, and three cost totals don't reconcile. Period: 2021–2028.

**What repeats**
- **The DGAL debt table 2014–2024**, in full, twice: Orçamentado "A série do regulador" (4 of 11 rows receipted) and Quinze Anos "A dívida vista de fora" (none receipted). Part of it appears again on the municipality page.
- **The 2024 vote (2–5) and the auditor's impossibility declaration**: in Orçamentado and in Quinze Anos ("O essencial", "2021–2025 — CDU, em minoria"). They give different causes: "A votação ocorre meses antes das eleições" against "a divisão de mandatos de 2021 lida em voz alta".
- **137 days, €4,976,172, €54,379,035**: sealed in Orçamentado, unsealed in Quinze Anos "O essencial". Both index summaries lead with 137.
- **The 2026 budget (€110,840,577)** in four studies: Orçamentado "O que 2026 diz que vai fazer", Quinze Anos "O que o plano de 2026 diz", Economia "Quem meteu dinheiro a sério", Évora 2027 "Os documentos previsionais do município".
- **2025 plan execution (€27,369,083)**: Orçamentado, and Pelouros "Os três anos de charneira".
- **Population, firms, purchasing power, overnight stays**: Quinze Anos "População, trabalho, empresas e visitantes", and Economia.

1. **PRR totals.**
   - Orçamentado "O dinheiro prometido de fora": "a 2026-08-04, diz … € 167 372 756; pago, € 86 944 669".
   - Prometido "O que foi prometido e o que foi pago" gives the same pair at "data de referência 2026-08-19". The municipality page's "O que mudou" dates the pair to 20.08.2026.
   - Economia "Quem meteu dinheiro a sério" has a 2026-08-07 snapshot (167 337 246; 84 368 019; "603 localizações com 102 678 704"). Prometido "O que significa «dentro do prazo»" has "608 localizações … € 102 624 704".
2. **Hospital equipment.** Economia "Quem meteu dinheiro a sério": "com dinheiro do PRR a comprar-lhe agora o equipamento médico pesado". Prometido "O que significa «dentro do prazo»": €5,314,868 approved, €0 paid.
3. **Évora 2027 in the 2026 budget.**
   - Évora 2027 "O que não se encontrou": the file is a scan, "os valores não se transcrevem".
   - Orçamentado "O que 2026 diz que vai fazer": "€ 3 984 060 para as obras do «Évora 2027»".
   - Quinze Anos "O que o plano de 2026 diz": "€ 7 470 000", and in the annex "€ 14 798 219 †".
4. **Earnings.** Economia "Os limites": "os ganhos param em 2022". Quinze Anos "População, trabalho…" runs to 2024 (€1,484).
5. **The 2009–2013 mandate.** Pelouros "Oito limites": "O presidente desse mandato … não estão registados". Quinze Anos "Os cinco mandatos, e os dois anteriores" names both presidents. The municipality page prints both claims on one card.
6. **Debt direction.**
   - Orçamentado "O que concluir disto": "a composição da dívida piora mesmo enquanto o total desce".
   - Quinze Anos "O essencial": "com a descida parada"; and "2021–2025": "A dívida subiu em 2024".
   - Orçamentado "A série do regulador" has the municipality's and the regulator's figures agreeing (€926 apart in 2024). Quinze Anos "A dívida vista de fora" shows gaps of €4.3–5.4M in 2020–2023.
7. **Supplier days, 2022**: 69 in Orçamentado "Fornecedores", 64 in Quinze Anos "As finanças municipais".
8. **A stale correction.** Pelouros "Fontes e lacunas" corrects Quinze Anos for lacking a 2021–2023 culture line. Quinze Anos "A cultura…" now prints that line.

**Titles that confuse**
- "Orçamentado, Pago, Devido 2025" and "Prometido, Pago, Auditado 2026" share a pattern. "Auditado" means the Tribunal de Contas catalogue, while the audit of the accounts is in the other study.
- "Prometido … 2026" and "Évora 2027: o prometido …" read as one series.
- "Quinze Anos" and "Os Pelouros … O Que Fizeram" both go mandate by mandate, and "Quinze Anos" is mostly finance.

## The coherent set

Four studies. The split uses Quinze Anos's own rule, "Um partido é dono das suas decisões, não de uma curva": acts with a named author go in one study, unattributed series in another.

**A. As contas da Câmara de Évora, 2010–2025: orçamento, cobrança, dívida e certificação**
- **Question:** can the câmara pay its way, and how did the rescue-era debt get to where it is?
- **Sections, in order:**
  1. limits
  2. essentials
  3. 2025 (Orçamentado's sections 2–6)
  4. fifteen years of accounts and staff
  5. the inherited debt and the excess over the ceiling
  6. debt in three layers
  7. the regulator's series with the reconciliation
  8. the auditor, with 2024 told once
  9. culture spending
  10. the 2026 budget and plan
  11. conclusions
- **Cut:** the second DGAL table and the second 2024 account; Orçamentado's PRR paragraph; Quinze Anos's restatement of 2025.

**B. Quem governou a Câmara de Évora, 2009–2025: eleições, executivos, pelouros e decisões**
- **Question:** who governed, with what majority, who held which portfolio, and what did each executive decide?
- **Sections, in order:**
  1. limits
  2. essentials
  3. elections 2001–2025 (assembly, parishes, count discrepancies)
  4. changes of president
  5. mandate by mandate: seats, pelouros, dated decisions (PAEL, saneamento, water agreements, refinancing, IMI)
  6. concentration
  7. pelouros against the accounts
  8. conclusions
- **Cut:** Quinze Anos's "O que herdou"/"O que deixou" paragraphs (they are A's series); Pelouros's correction note.

**C. A economia de Évora e o dinheiro público que lhe chega por fora da câmara (agosto de 2026)**
- **Question:** what does the concelho produce, what outside public money reaches it, through whom, and how much is overdue?
- **Sections, in order:**
  1. limits
  2. essentials
  3. population and work 2009–2025
  4. firms, exports, tourism
  5. state contracts and EU funds
  6. the PRR
  7. contracts
  8. audits
  9. conclusions
  10. a dated annex on funding doors

**D. Évora 2027, Capital Europeia da Cultura: o que a candidatura prometeu, o que o painel europeu escreveu e o dinheiro nos atos públicos**
- Stands alone: it has its own subject, sources, method and next events (autumn and end of 2026). It only gains links.

**Reader's path.** From the municipality page:
