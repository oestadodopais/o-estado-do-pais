## The coherent set

**Four studies, with H redistributed.** Each opens with conclusions and ends with its method and receipts.

1. **Évora: contas, dívida e orçamento municipal.** Question: can the council finance its commitments? Order: latest accounts; receipts and spending; investment; suppliers and borrowing; historical debt and staffing; certification; the 2026 budget. Feed it A’s financial sections and H’s financial material from “Mandato a mandato”, financial/staffing subsections and budget section. Cut duplicate debt tables, bailout narratives and audit explanations. Move territorial PRR detail to study 3.

2. **Évora: eleições, executivos e pelouros.** Question: who governed, held responsibilities and made documented decisions? Order: elections and succession; executive composition; portfolio changes; correspondence with account functions; spending/activity examples; attribution limits. Combine H’s electoral sections and documented decisions with P’s “O mapa, mandato a mandato”, “A correspondência, declarada” and portfolio panels. Cut repeated executive lists and fiscal verdicts. Governance deserves independent treatment because duties and financial outcomes are different questions.

3. **Évora: economia, investimento e financiamento no concelho.** Question: what supports economic activity, and through whom does investment flow? Order: economic structure; trade/tourism; investment anchors; PRR beneficiaries and payments; deadlines; public contracts; audit traceability; dated funding opportunities. Combine E’s substantive sections, H’s socioeconomic series and R’s register investigations. Cut duplicated PRR summaries and CEC exposition; retain funding calls as an explicitly dated appendix.

4. **Évora 2027: candidatura, financiamento, obras e governação.** Question: what commitments exist, who owes delivery, and what remains unverified? Order: chronology; promises/panel assessments; funding by document; works/promoters; association governance; contracting evidence; unresolved gaps. Retain C’s core, absorbing relevant works material from H and the association’s partial BASE evidence from R. Remove E’s abbreviated CEC account. The association, programme and monitoring process justify a standalone study.

On the municipality page, those questions become the reading routes: council finances → 1; responsibility → 2; economy/investment → 3; cultural programme → 4. Current indicators remain the entry point.

## What is lost and what is risked

**Accounts/history merge:** historical debt, loans and staffing acquire a financial home; electoral decisions acquire a governance home. Keep their cross-links. Accounting breaks and municipal/DGAL disagreements prevent a seamless series. H’s “O que concluir disto” claim that “a minoria produziu” deterioration cannot survive as demonstrated causation.

**Government/portfolios merge:** electoral counts and portfolio assignments belong together; function costs remain municipal costs. The merge cannot create expenditure by individual. P’s “Fontes e lacunas” also requires rederiving correspondence from annual reports; early allocations remain incomplete.

**Economy/public-money merge:** business indicators, PRR totals and contracting evidence move together, with separate universes and dates. Approved aid, contract prices and municipal receipts cannot be added. E’s “Onde está a margem por explorar” claims about thin supply chains and public salaries/pensions need evidence absent from this package.

**Culture consolidation:** works figures change home without becoming a complete programme account. Move the association’s “39” contracts worth “1 997 619” euros from R, “Contratos públicos”, as a bounded window. Reconciliation still requires project-scope matching, the missing cooperation protocol and payment evidence identified in C’s “O que não se encontrou”.

All successors need explicit boundaries of entity, measure and date, a discrepancy table, and a section/claim/receipt migration map. Preserve ledger IDs; C has document receipts but no study-owned rows in `linhas-por-estudo.json`.

## What is fine

Keep A’s debt layers; P’s explicit, inferred, shared and unassigned mappings; R’s warnings against double counting and equating catalogue silence with no audit; and C’s document-by-document presentation. Keep missing values, accounting breaks and uncertainty marks visible.

Preserve all six original URLs and dated editions, with successor links and correction histories; promote the four successors on the index and municipality page. Retain the conclusions-first requirement, source-page receipts, method statements, AI disclosure, sober design and Portuguese editions with English mirrors.
